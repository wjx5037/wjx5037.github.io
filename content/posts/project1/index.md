## Project Goal

Develop a forklift-mounted attachment that can pick up, align, transport, and install semi-assembled hollow concrete guardrails with less manual handling and lower risk of surface damage.

## Engineering Requirements

- Mount directly to a forklift frame and remain practical to manufacture and maintain.
- Reach the guardrail cavity, establish repeatable alignment, and support the load during placement.
- Provide lateral positioning for installation tolerance in a construction environment.
- Protect the concrete with compliant contact surfaces rather than relying on hard steel-on-concrete contact.

{{< figure src="空心混凝土护栏.png" title="SolidWorks model of the semi-assembled hollow concrete guardrail" width="58%" >}}

## Mechanism Architecture

The selected transverse-gripping architecture combines a forklift-frame connection, a linear-bearing horizontal translation stage, a cylinder-actuated parallelogram linkage, a load-bearing lower arm, and an adaptive upper contact element.

- The lower arm enters the hollow section and its locating feature references the guardrail center opening.
- The parallelogram linkage keeps the upper and lower contacts coordinated as the gripping cylinder closes.
- The horizontal cylinder provides final lateral adjustment before installation.
- Rubber pads on the lower arm and adaptive contact reduce local indentation and abrasion.

{{< figure src="figure1.png" title="Overall SolidWorks assembly showing the forklift mount, translation stage, linkage, and adaptive contact" >}}

## Operating Sequence

- Translate the attachment to align the lower arm with the guardrail cavity.
- Insert and locate the lower arm at the center opening.
- Retract the gripping cylinder to clamp the guardrail between the lower support and adaptive upper contact.
- Transport and laterally position the guardrail; reverse the sequence to release it after installation.

## My Contribution

I proposed and refined the transverse-gripping concept, completed the full SolidWorks assembly, and used motion studies to verify the insertion, clamping, and release sequence. I also prepared and submitted the national invention-patent documentation for the attachment.

## Design Value

The mechanism turns the guardrail cavity into a reliable handling interface: it provides support, alignment, controlled clamping, and compliant contact in one forklift-compatible attachment. The resulting concept improves installation repeatability while protecting a brittle, high-value precast component.

## Tech Stack

SolidWorks CAD, mechanism design, cylinder-actuated linkage design, motion simulation, design-for-manufacturing, and patent documentation.
